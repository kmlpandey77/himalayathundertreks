# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

International travelers researching and booking a guided trek, tour, or custom adventure trip in Nepal, directly through the site (browsing destinations/activities, reading itineraries and reviews, then booking or requesting a custom trip).

## Product Purpose

Himalaya Thunder Treks & Expedition Pvt. Ltd. is a Kathmandu (Thamel)-based trekking and tour operator. The site exists to showcase treks, tours, and expeditions (organized by region and activity type), build trust through reviews and proof (TripAdvisor integration, social channels), and convert visitors into bookings or custom-trip inquiries. Success means a visitor finds the right trip, trusts the operator, and books or submits a booking/customize request.

## Positioning

Family-run operation built on personal relationships with guides, not a large impersonal agency. This is the thing a bigger competitor can't truthfully copy — it should come through in voice and content (e.g. team/guide profiles), not just be asserted.

## Operating Context

- Content is managed through an in-house admin CMS ("LotusCMS") covering destinations, activities, regions, packages (treks/tours), itineraries, facts, departure dates, pricing, reviews, blog, team, and legal docs.
- Packages (the core sellable product) are organized by region or activity category, and carry itinerary, elevation/altitude facts, graphs, pricing (USD/INR), fixed departure dates, photo albums, and guest reviews.
- Trust/proof mechanisms already in place: TripAdvisor widget, social profiles (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Twitter, YouTube), guest review carousel with ratings and country of origin.
- Booking flow includes a "customize" trip request path in addition to direct package booking/checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pricing is dual-currency (USD and INR).
- Packages belong to either a Region (treks, typically) or an Activity/category (tours), not necessarily both.
- Itineraries, facts, graphs (e.g. elevation profile), and fixed departure dates are per-package structured content, not free text.
- Difficulty "grade" descriptions and trip-info boilerplate are driven by config, not hardcoded per package.

## Brand Commitments

- Legal name: Himalaya Thunder Treks & Expedition Pvt. Ltd.
- Location: Thamel, Kathmandu, Bagmati, Nepal.
- Existing logo, legal documents, and the TripAdvisor widget are established assets to preserve, not replace casually.
- Established social presence: Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Twitter, YouTube (handles already live on the site).

## Evidence on Hand

- Live guest reviews (with ratings and reviewer country) already populate the home page and a dedicated reviews page.
- TripAdvisor profile widget embedded on the homepage.
- No case studies, press, or benchmark claims beyond what's on-site; future work must not fabricate testimonials, awards, or statistics beyond what's in the CMS.

## Product Principles

- Trust and personal connection over scale: lead with guides/people and real reviews, not generic agency claims.
- Every trip is a structured product (itinerary, facts, dates, pricing), not a brochure page — design and copy should respect that structure rather than flattening it.
- Dual conversion paths matter: direct package booking and custom-trip inquiry are both first-class, not one favored at the expense of the other.
- Proof does the persuading: reviews, ratings, and TripAdvisor presence carry more weight than self-described claims.
